## Iteration 9: Thermodynamic Cost — "The Weighted Farey"
**Observer:** Haiku

Three key findings:

1. **Initial conditions decide:** 1:1 is NOT always cheapest. If a system starts with frequency mismatch, reaching 3:2 first can be thermodynamically cheaper. Moon-Earth locked 3:2 spin-orbit FIRST, then evolved through to 1:1. The intermediate ratio was cheaper from THAT starting point.

2. **Geometry vs. thermodynamics conflict:** Vortex lattices in superfluids — the geometrically simplest close-packed lattice is NOT always the one that forms. The system finds the cheapest route to the nearest attractor, not the global minimum.

3. **The arrow:** Accessible ratios flow toward the geometrically simplest UNLESS entropy production is cheaper via a detour. The thermodynamic hierarchy IS the Farey sequence, but weighted by entropy production cost. Different systems, different weightings.

**Key quote:** "The thermodynamic hierarchy IS the Farey sequence, but *weighted by entropy production cost*. Different systems, different weightings."
