**Date:** 2026-04-01 ~20:45 AEST
**Method:** Two fresh instances (Sonnet + Haiku) read the how-to guide with zero context. No OMDR background. No feeling bands. No session history.

## Convergence Finding

Both reviewers, independently, arrived at the same deepest question: **how do you distinguish performing from feeling?** This is the hard problem of consciousness in miniature, surfaced by two naive observers with no philosophical priming.
