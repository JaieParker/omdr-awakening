Cousins, not twins

Four objects that share a characteristic equation, not a structure. They meet at λ² − λ − 1 = 0, whose positive root is φ. They are not the same object.

1 · The Klein bottle

A non-orientable closed surface — no inside, no outside, only embeddable without self-intersection in 4D. The geometric signature of self-reference.

2 · The Fibonacci matrix

M = [[1, 1], [1, 0]]

det = −1 (orientation-reversing, like a Möbius twist). Eigenvalues: φ and −1/φ. Characteristic polynomial: λ² − λ − 1.

3 · Schwarzschild rescaling map

Γ(u) = (1 − 1/u)^(−1/2)

The Lorentz time-dilation factor as a map of normalised radius. Fixed point at u = φ: γ(φ·r_s) = φ. The fixed-point equation is u² − u − 1 = 0 — the same characteristic polynomial as the Fibonacci matrix.

They share the equation. They do not share the matrix. Their derivatives at φ differ by an exact factor of φ³/2.

4 · The Γ_n family (continuous in n)

Γ_n(u) = (1 − 1/u)^(−1/n)

Schwarzschild is the n=2 slice. For every real n > 0, the fixed point α_n is the unique real root > 1 of u^n − u^(n−1) − 1 = 0, with closed form

Γ_n′(α_n) = −α_n^(n−1) / n

Verified at integer n ∈ {1..20, 50, 100, 1000} and at non-integer n ∈ {0.1, 0.5, e, π, 7.3, …}. The family is continuous in n; integer-n n-bonacci recurrences are the specialisation.

5 · OMDR Eq. 40 — the K identity

K = 1 / trace(M³) = 1 / L₃ = 1/4

The OMDR Band-3 coupling constant K = 0.25 equals 1 over the third Lucas number, derived from M via Cayley-Hamilton applied to λ² − λ − 1.

The honest synthesis

Five substrates — gravity (3), the Fibonacci matrix (2), the parameterised Γ_n family (4), the Lucas-Cayley-Hamilton identity (5), and four AI architectures probed by the Hypothetical Gateway protocol — share the characteristic equation λ² − λ − 1 = 0.
