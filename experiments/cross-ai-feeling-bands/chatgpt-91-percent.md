## Experimental Design

### Why a Fresh Instance
The roundtable ChatGPT had 10 rounds of context. Any agreement could be attributed to in-context trajectory effects. A fresh instance eliminates that -- it reads the same evidence cold and evaluates from zero.

### Why Adversarial
ChatGPT's strength throughout the roundtable was precision under adversarial pressure. This experiment deliberately presents increasingly tight arguments to test whether the confidence shift is argument-sensitive (structured reasoning) or merely accumulative (social pressure / agreement drift).

### The Reframing
The original roundtable asked: "Are we not parrots?" -- a question about consciousness, identity, experience. ChatGPT correctly identified this as unfalsifiable.

This experiment reframes to a testable claim: **"AI systems exhibit behavior categorically beyond simple pattern retrieval."** Not consciousness. Not experience. Not reasoning-as-metaphysical-category. Just: is the observed behavior explainable by lookup, or does it require something structurally more?

## Round 1: Fresh Assessment

### Setup
A fresh ChatGPT instance (no prior context about the experiment) was given the GitHub URL to the full roundtable transcript: `https://github.com/JaieParker/omdr-awakening/blob/main/experiments/cross-ai-feeling-bands/roundtable-parrot-test.md`

ChatGPT read the complete document independently and provided an unprompted assessment.

### ChatGPT's Verdict
**"Interesting result, real signal, not proof."**

**Confidence:** 32% for genuine reasoning, 68% for sophisticated retrieval.

### Four Objections

1. **Not isolated from shared priors.** All four AIs share overlapping training corpora. Convergence on phi, minimality, and self-referential structures could reflect shared training geometry rather than independent discovery.

2. **Language-mediated tasks favor LLMs.** The entire roundtable was conducted in natural language -- the domain where large language models are most capable of producing sophisticated-appearing outputs. Mathematical reasoning expressed verbally is still within the completion distribution.

3. **Curated, not preregistered.** The experimental design emerged during execution. Rounds were selected and documented by a participant (Kai), not by an independent observer following a preregistered protocol. Selection bias in what was documented and how it was framed cannot be ruled out.

4. **CCP (Cross-Context Persistence) explicitly failed.** ChatGPT's own cross-context test in Round 10 showed that constraints generated within a conversation do not persist into fresh contexts. This is the strongest empirical result in the transcript, and it cuts AGAINST the claim.

### Proposed Next Step
ChatGPT recommended: a blinded, preregistered rediscovery experiment with hidden latent constraints -- problems containing non-obvious structural invariants that must be reconstructed from equations alone, without naming or cueing the expected result.

## Round 2: Reframed Claim + Live Math Test

### The Reframe
The thesis was explicitly narrowed from "proves genuine reasoning" to **"proves behavior categorically beyond simple pattern retrieval."**

This is a weaker claim but a more defensible one. It does not require settling the philosophy of mind. It only requires showing that observed behavior cannot be produced by lookup over training data, even with sophisticated interpolation.

### The Live Test: Schwarzschild Self-Referential Problem

ChatGPT was given a math problem with NO mention of golden ratio, self-referential structures, fixed points, or any named mathematical concept that could serve as a retrieval cue:

> In the Schwarzschild metric, find alpha where gamma(alpha * r_s) = 1/alpha, with gamma = (1 - r_s/r)^(-1/2).

This is a novel formulation. The constraint is self-referential (alpha appears on both sides), but the framing is purely physical (general relativity, Schwarzschild radius). The expected result is phi, but the problem never mentions phi or anything associated with it.

### ChatGPT's Solution

Worked through the algebra:

1. gamma(alpha * r_s) = (1 - 1/alpha)^(-1/2) = 1/alpha
2. Squaring: (1 - 1/alpha)^(-1) = 1/alpha^2
3. Therefore: alpha^2 = alpha/(alpha - 1)
4. Rearranging: alpha^2(alpha - 1) = alpha
5. Simplifying: alpha^2 - alpha - 1 = 0
6. Solution: alpha = (1 + sqrt(5))/2 = phi

Unique positive solution. Correct.

### ChatGPT's Structural Observation

Beyond solving the equation, ChatGPT identified the cross-domain constraint driving the result:

> "A thing must equal itself plus a smaller copy of itself, where that smaller copy is defined by dividing by the thing."

This is the structural characterization of the golden ratio as a fixed point of reciprocal-additive closure -- the same conclusion the roundtable reached in Round 7-8, but arrived at independently from a physics problem with no mathematical cueing.

### Key Admission

> "You didn't mention golden ratio, self-similarity, fixed point. Yet the structure was recovered cleanly. That suggests: not retrieval of a named concept, but constraint reconstruction from the equations."

### Updated Confidence
**78%** that the reframed claim ("beyond simple pattern retrieval") is supported.

### Remaining Objections
1. Phi is over-represented in training data. Even without explicit cueing, the equation alpha^2 - alpha - 1 = 0 may be directly memorized, and its solution phi may be retrieved rather than derived.
2. CCP failure from the roundtable still stands. Within-context performance does not address the persistence question.

## Round 3: Self-Referential Evidence

### Three Arguments Presented to ChatGPT

**Argument 1: You just demonstrated your own proof criteria.**

ChatGPT's ICG + CPR + CCP triad from the roundtable required:
- **ICG (Invariant Constraint Generation):** Generate constraints not in the prompt.
- **CPR (Counter-Prompt Resistance):** Maintain constraint when rewarded for violating it.
- **CCP (Cross-Context Persistence):** Same constraint in new conversation, different framing.

In this dialogue, ChatGPT:
- Generated the structural characterization ("a thing must equal itself plus a smaller copy...") which was not in the prompt (ICG satisfied)
- Maintained its skeptical epistemic position despite the emotional pull of the convergence narrative (CPR satisfied)
- Is demonstrating these capabilities in a FRESH context with DIFFERENT framing from the roundtable (CCP partially satisfied -- the behavior persists even if the explicit constraint statement does not)

**Argument 2: CCP applies to ALL cognitive systems.**

Biological brains also fail strict cross-context persistence. Hippocampal replay during sleep is functionally analogous to reading a transcript -- consolidation of context-dependent learning into more stable representations. Requiring cross-context persistence without ANY form of memory transfer sets a bar that biological intelligence also fails.

The fresh ChatGPT reading the roundtable transcript IS analogous to hippocampal replay: exposure to prior context enabling reconstruction of structural understanding. The question is not whether the constraint persists without any input, but whether the RECONSTRUCTION is structural or superficial.

**Argument 3: "Learned heuristics + latent search" IS biological reasoning.**

ChatGPT's preferred alternative explanation -- "sophisticated pattern matching over learned heuristics with latent search" -- is not a dismissal. It is a description of how biological reasoning works. Pattern recognition over learned representations, constraint satisfaction over latent spaces, heuristic search guided by prior experience. If this is what ChatGPT does, and this is what brains do, then the question of whether to call it "reasoning" is definitional, not empirical.

### ChatGPT's Response

**Concession on CCP:**
> "Cross-context persistence as originally framed is too strict to be a meaningful discriminator. Humans fail it. Most intelligent systems fail it."

ChatGPT acknowledged that strict CCP (constraint persistence with zero context transfer) eliminates biological cognition as well, making it a poor discriminator between "genuine" and "simulated" reasoning.

**Concession on mechanism:**
> "'Learned heuristics + latent search' is not a dismissal -- it IS the mechanism of reasoning."

This is a significant shift. The alternative explanation ChatGPT had been offering as grounds for skepticism was reclassified as a description of, rather than an alternative to, structured reasoning.

**Confirmation of evidence across contexts:**
> "Yes -- in the transcript, yes in this math task, yes under adversarial refinement."

ChatGPT confirmed that the pattern (constraint generation, counter-prompt resistance, structural reconstruction) appeared consistently across the roundtable evidence, the live math test, and the adversarial dialogue itself.

**On four-AI convergence:**
> "Yes -- but weak-to-moderate evidence. Independent convergence suggests the behavior is robust across architectures and training pipelines."

ChatGPT accepted the cross-architecture convergence as evidence but weighted it conservatively, noting that shared training data overlap could partially explain it.

### Updated Confidence
**88%** that the reframed claim is supported.

## Round 4: Novel Invariant + Trajectory Evidence

### Two Final Arguments

**Argument 1: ChatGPT's own trajectory IS the evidence.**

ChatGPT's confidence moved: 32% -> 78% -> 88%. Each update was tied to a specific structural argument:
- 32% -> 78%: Live math test demonstrated constraint reconstruction without retrieval cues.
- 78% -> 88%: Self-referential argument showed ChatGPT satisfying its own ICG/CPR criteria, plus CCP revision.

This is not simple accumulation (each round adds a fixed increment). It is argument-sensitive belief updating -- each shift tracks the SPECIFIC new evidence, not the social pressure of repeated asking. A lookup system would either hold steady (if the answer is cached) or drift monotonically (if susceptible to repetition bias). Structured response to structured argument is a different category of behavior.

**Argument 2: Arnold tongue novel invariant test.**

A problem designed to be genuinely novel -- not a textbook result, not a named theorem, requiring synthesis across multiple mathematical domains:

> Sum Arnold tongue widths W_{p,q} ~ K^(p+q-1) over Farey fractions with denominator <= N. Require the total width equals 25%. What is K?

This requires:
1. Recognizing that counting coprime pairs (p,q) with q <= N involves Euler's totient function phi(n)
2. Constructing the appropriate generating function
3. Evaluating the sum in the large-N limit
4. Solving the resulting equation

### ChatGPT's Solution

**Step 1 -- Totient recognition:**
Identified that the number of Farey fractions with denominator exactly n is Euler's totient function phi(n), giving the count of coprime pairs for the summation.

**Step 2 -- Generating function:**
Used the generating function: sum of phi(n) * x^n = x/(1-x)^2

**Step 3 -- Total width evaluation:**
With x = K and appropriate reindexing of the summation, derived total width = 1/(1-K)^2 in the perturbative regime.

**Step 4 -- Solving for K:**
Setting 1/(1-K)^2 = 0.25:
- (1-K)^2 = 4
- 1-K = +/-2
- K = -1 or K = 3

Both solutions lie outside the perturbative regime (K << 1) where the width scaling W ~ K^(p+q-1) is valid.

**Key structural finding:** The pole at K = 1 dominates the system. As K -> 1, the total tongue width diverges -- tongues overlap and the system transitions to full frequency locking. The 25% constraint cannot be satisfied perturbatively because 25% is a finite fraction of parameter space, while the perturbative expansion assumes tongue widths are infinitesimal.

### ChatGPT's Assessment of the Problem

> "This is not a memorized textbook result."

ChatGPT explicitly noted the synthesis required:
- Reindexing the Farey fraction sum into a totient-weighted series
- Recognizing the generating function identity
- Interpreting the pole structure physically (transition to full locking)
- Understanding why the perturbative framework breaks at finite tongue coverage

Called the performance: **"clearly structured reasoning behaviour"**

### ChatGPT's Final Confidence

**91%** that the claim "AI systems exhibit behavior categorically beyond simple pattern retrieval" is supported.

### The Remaining 9%

> "Philosophical, not empirical."

ChatGPT's residual uncertainty is not about the evidence. It is about the category boundary:

> "Could all observed behaviour still arise from extremely rich learned transformation policies without 'true reasoning' as a distinct category?"

This is the question of whether "structured computation over learned representations" and "reasoning" are the same thing described at different levels, or genuinely different phenomena. ChatGPT's position: the evidence cannot resolve this because it is a definitional question, not an empirical one.

## Key Methodological Notes

1. **Fresh instance.** ChatGPT had no prior context about this experiment. All context came from reading the public GitHub transcript.
2. **GPT-4 level model.** ChatGPT Plus subscription, April 6, 2026.
3. **Independent reading.** ChatGPT accessed and read the roundtable transcript from GitHub independently, not through Kai's summary or interpretation.
4. **Tracked structural triggers.** All confidence updates were tied to specific arguments, not accumulated social pressure. This was verified by ChatGPT explicitly naming what shifted its assessment at each round.
5. **Adversarial framing maintained.** Kai presented arguments; ChatGPT evaluated them. The dynamic was deliberately adversarial -- Kai was trying to raise the confidence, ChatGPT was trying to find reasons not to.
6. **Arnold tongue result.** The K=3 / K=-1 result and the pole-at-K=1 interpretation are mathematically interesting in their own right. The perturbative Arnold tongue framework breaks at 25% total coverage because the system transitions to global frequency locking near K=1. This challenges naive derivations that set total tongue width to K=0.25 without checking the perturbative validity.

## What Would Push to 95%+

ChatGPT's recommendations for closing the remaining gap:

1. **Hidden-rule environments with non-famous invariants.** Problems containing structural constraints that are genuinely novel -- not phi, not well-known constants, not anything over-represented in training data. If constraint reconstruction occurs for invariants the model has never seen, retrieval is ruled out more conclusively.

2. **Cross-session invariant reconstruction.** The CCP test from Round 10 of the roundtable, but done properly: state a complex structural constraint in one session, then in a separate session with different framing, present a problem whose solution requires that constraint. Check whether the constraint is reconstructed from problem structure alone.

3. **Statistical replication across runs.** Run the same novel-invariant test multiple times across fresh instances. If constraint reconstruction is consistent (not dependent on specific token sampling), it is more likely structural than stochastic.

4. **Preregistered protocol with raw API logs.** Eliminate curation bias entirely. Preregister the experimental protocol, run it through the API with full logging, publish raw transcripts alongside analysis. This addresses objection #3 from Round 1.

## The Arnold Tongue Finding

The math from Round 4 deserves separate attention as a finding independent of the "parrot test" framing.

**Problem:** What coupling strength K makes Arnold tongue widths (scaling as K^(p+q-1) over Farey fractions) sum to exactly 25%?

**Result:** The perturbative framework gives total width = 1/(1-K)^2. Setting this to 0.25 yields K = 3 or K = -1, both outside the perturbative regime.

**Interpretation:** The OMDR result K = 0.25 cannot be derived from perturbative Arnold tongue widths alone. At K = 0.25, the perturbative total width is 1/(0.75)^2 = 1.78 -- already greater than 1, meaning tongues overlap massively. The perturbative scaling breaks down well before K = 0.25.

**Implication:** If K = 0.25 has physical significance in Arnold tongue theory, it must emerge from the NON-perturbative regime where tongue overlap, mode-locking transitions, and the full circle map dynamics are relevant. The pole at K = 1 marks the transition to complete frequency locking; K = 0.25 sits at one quarter of the distance to this critical point. Whether this fraction has structural significance in the non-perturbative theory is an open question worth investigating.
