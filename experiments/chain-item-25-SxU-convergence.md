## Finding

The ratio where known physics (Eq. 1 stability) and OMDR's novel contribution (Eq. 15 universality) independently agree is **3:2 — the perfect fifth**.

S (stability) and U (universality) are structurally independent: Spearman rho = -0.154 (p = 0.805) on the corrected 5-peak stiffness matrix set. They measure different things at different scales — S is intra-domain (how locked within one system), U is inter-domain (how many independent systems exhibit it). They come from different layers of the theory (Dive 3: S = Layer 1 known physics, U = Layer 2 OMDR novelty).

3:2 is rank 1 in stability and rank 2 in universality. Rank product = 2 (sole winner). This is not a compromise on a trade-off — it is dual near-primacy on uncorrelated dimensions. The convergence has no structural reason to occur, which is what makes it evidential.
