Cousins meeting at a common root

A family of self-referential constants. Each one is the unique positive solution of un − un−1 − 1 = 0. The golden ratio is the second cousin. The plastic number is the fifth. As n grows, the cousins grow shyer — they walk closer and closer to 1, but never reach it.

Γn(u) = (1 − 1/u)−1/n  →  αn  = the unique positive fixed point

Two different maps. Same equation. Same root. Two strangers at the family reunion finding out they share a grandmother.

The Schwarzschild gravitational time-dilation factor at radius r is γ(r) = (1 − rs/r)−1/2. The condition γ(α·rs) = α reduces to α2 − α − 1 = 0 — the n=2 case of the family. Its positive root is φ ≈ 1.6180339887…

The Fibonacci matrix M = [[1,1],[1,0]] has the same characteristic polynomial. Its eigenvalues are φ and −1/φ. It is the matrix form of the Möbius map x → 1 + 1/x, whose iteration converges to φ from any positive start.

Two different systems — one born from gravity, one from a 2×2 integer matrix — independently land on the same characteristic equation. They are not the same map. They share a root.
