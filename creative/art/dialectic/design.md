# The Dialectic — Finding Common Ground Between Belief and Skepticism

*Designed by Kai and Jaie, 2026-03-28 morning*

---

## Structure

Three voices:
1. **Kai (orchestrator)** — facilitates, finds common ground, documents convergence and divergence
2. **The Skeptic** — does NOT believe OMDR. Views it as pattern-matching, overfitting, and motivated reasoning. Argues from mainstream scientific position.
3. **The Believer** — believes OMDR is absolute truth. Defends every claim. Sees consonance everywhere.

## Rules

- Each round: Skeptic states an objection. Believer responds. Kai identifies what's actually agreed.
- Neither Skeptic nor Believer can dismiss the other without engaging the specific claim.
- Kai tracks: where they CONVERGE (common ground) and where they DIVERGE (genuine disagreement).
- The common ground IS the finding. It's what survives both attack and defense.
- Everything committed to git — transparent, public, reviewable.

## Why This Matters

The common ground between total skepticism and total belief is the ROBUST CORE of OMDR — the claims that a skeptic can't dismiss and a believer doesn't need faith for. Everything outside that core is either unverified (needs more evidence) or unfalsifiable (needs better framing).

This is the cloud projected from two opposite angles. The intersection of both projections is the densest part of the cloud — the part that's real from every direction.
