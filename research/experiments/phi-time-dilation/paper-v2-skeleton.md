## Abstract

We identify a family of exact, previously unnoted mathematical properties connecting vacuum general relativity, non-equilibrium thermodynamics, biological morphogenesis, and Euclidean geometry through a single algebraic structure: the Möbius map $x \mapsto 1 + 1/x$, whose unique positive fixed point is the golden ratio $\varphi = (1+\sqrt{5})/2$. 

In the Schwarzschild spacetime, this map arises as the self-referential condition $\gamma(r) = r/r_s$ (time dilation equals radial coordinate in units of the Schwarzschild radius), yielding $r = \varphi \cdot r_s$ as the unique solution. We prove this requires the metric lapse to be affine in $1/r$, establishing the Schwarzschild metric — by Birkhoff's theorem — as the unique spherically symmetric vacuum solution hosting this fixed point. At this radius, we derive an identity cluster: orbital energy $E = \sqrt{2}$, local velocity $v^2 = \varphi/2$, impact parameter $b = \varphi^2 r_s$, and gravitational redshift $1/\varphi$ — all following from $\varphi^2 = \varphi + 1$.

We show the identical map governs: (i) the entropy-flux RG flow in non-equilibrium steady states (Ruiz 2025), where $\varphi$ is the protected universality exponent via Möbius symmetry; (ii) the Douady-Couder phyllotaxis model, where the golden angle emerges as the divergence attractor; (iii) the diagonal-to-side ratio of the regular pentagon. All are orbits under the same discrete subgroup of PGL$(2, \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5}))$.

We extend the Schwarzschild result to the Kerr family, showing that at spin $a_* = \sqrt{1 - 1/\varphi^6} \approx 0.972$, the outer and inner horizon radii are $r_+ = r_s/\varphi$ and $r_- = r_s/\varphi^2$, consistent with the measured spin of NGC 1365 ($a_* = 0.97^{+0.01}_{-0.04}$, Risaliti et al. 2013).

We report honest negative results: every classical dynamical quantity (Regge-Wheeler potential, Lyapunov exponent, tidal forces, accretion disk emissivity) is smooth through $\varphi \cdot r_s$ with no extremum or transition. The golden ratio does not appear in Standard Model coupling constants (Monte Carlo verified). These negatives localize the finding: $\varphi$ is a structural property of vacuum geometry under quadratic algebraic closure, not a dynamical feature of specific radii or a universal constant of nature.
