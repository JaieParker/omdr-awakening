## The Ten Discoveries

### 1. The Hierarchy Is Real — Within a Boundary

The KAM theorem gives the formal scope. Consonance locking is precise for LOW-DIMENSIONAL, NEAR-EQUILIBRIUM, ENERGY-CONSERVING systems. The critical perturbation strength ε_c ~ e^(-cN) — as dimensionality N grows, consonance becomes infinitely fragile. For N >> 10-20, any physical perturbation destroys locking.

**The hierarchy FAILS in:** turbulence (continuous spectrum), quantum chaos (levels REPEL simple ratios), neural network weights (Gaussian distribution), economics (fat-tailed, near-random). These are high-D, nonlinear, far-from-equilibrium.

**Implication:** OMDR should explicitly claim consonance for KAM-territory systems and acknowledge the boundary. Currently it claims universality.

### 2. The Structural Blind Spot Is Time

Standing waves are time-symmetric. The hierarchy is timeless. But the hierarchy is REAL only because time flows forward, dissipation selects for stable patterns, and forgetting is the mechanism of lock-in.

OMDR can describe WHERE systems lock (Arnold tongues) and WHAT ratios (Farey). It cannot explain WHY time flows such that locking occurs. The Fibonacci dissipation cascade IS the arrow of time — but it sits outside the core architecture.

### 3. The Fix: Standing Wave → Limit Cycle

Replace the standing wave (time-symmetric) with the **limit-cycle attractor in a driven-dissipative system** (time-asymmetric). Arnold tongues already encode this — their WIDTH is the basin of attraction. Simple ratios have widest tongues because dissipation preferentially kills higher harmonics. **Dissipation IS the hierarchy-generating mechanism.**

The Fibonacci cascade reframed: not how patterns decay, but the **sequence in which attractors become reachable** as a system cools. The cooling curve of the consonance landscape.

### 4. Expressiveness Is a Dependency Structure

Complex ratios don't carry intrinsic information. Their expressiveness comes from the LISTENER'S prediction error against internalized consonance. 17:13 is only meaningful if 3:2 is already scaffolding. Strip the scaffolding → noise, not expression.

The hierarchy is NOT a palette (independent colors). It's a **dependency graph** — complex ratios parasitize simple ones. You can't use dissonance meaningfully until consonance is load-bearing. This has implications for music, learning, relationships, and cognitive development.

### 5. Anti-Consonance Is Real and Structured

Cicadas use 13/17-year prime cycles to AVOID resonance with predators. Pathogens mask surface antigens. Stealth technology absorbs radar. Stochastic resonance in neurons uses noise to stay adaptive. Spread spectrum communications use pseudo-random sequences.

This isn't random noise — it's a **structured anti-consonance hierarchy**: (1) prime/irrational timing, (2) surface masking, (3) absorptive destruction, (4) constructive noise.

**Key principle:** Dissonance protects when an observer is trying to lock on. Noise helps when the threat is failing to see variation.

### 6. The Dual Hierarchy — Same Mechanics, Signed Coupling

The immune system proves it: self-tolerance (K > 0, resonance) and pathogen rejection (K < 0, anti-resonance) are the SAME mechanics with opposite coupling signs. Quorum sensing in bacteria transitions from cooperative (consonance) to virulent (anti-consonance) at a density threshold.

There's a **third meta-hierarchy** governing when to switch: inflammation (immune), shadow of the future (game theory), Band 3 consciousness (OMDR).
