## Cycle 4 Finding: The Surprise Criterion — Where OMDR Sits on the Spectrum

### The Historical Pattern

Three historical parallels, stripped to mechanism:

**Quantum mechanics (wave math → particles):**
- **Metaphor phase (1924):** de Broglie says particles "behave like" waves. λ = h/p.
- **Model phase (1927):** Davisson-Germer confirms electron diffraction at predicted wavelengths. Schrödinger equation generates quantitative predictions no classical model can match.
- **Identity phase (~1930s onward):** The wave function IS the fundamental object. "Particle" becomes the approximation. The formalism is indispensable — you can't do atomic physics without it.

**What moved it:** Each transition required SURPRISE. Not just confirmed predictions, but predictions that surprised physicists AND couldn't be generated by the competing framework. Electron diffraction wasn't just "consistent with" wave theory — it was inexplicable without it. The formalism became indispensable because nothing else worked.

**Freud's hydraulic model (pressure physics → psyche):**
- **Metaphor phase (1895):** Psychic energy "behaves like" pressure in a system. Repression is a dam. Dreams are pressure release.
- **Never reached model phase.** Why? Because every observation was COMPATIBLE with the model but none was PREDICTED by it uniquely. Dream about water? Repressed sexuality. Dream about fire? Also repressed sexuality. The formalism generated descriptions, not surprise. Any outcome was "consistent with" the model.
- **Abandoned** when cognitive science generated actual surprise (e.g., specific memory retrieval patterns, specific neural correlates of fear conditioning).

**What killed it:** Zero surprise. The framework redescribed known phenomena without predicting unknown ones. No observation could falsify it because the interpreter always had freedom to fit.

**Thermodynamics (molecular motion → heat):**
- **Metaphor phase:** Heat "behaves as if" it were molecular motion.
- **Model phase:** Specific heat predictions. Gas law quantitative match. Brownian motion predicted AND confirmed (Einstein 1905, Perrin 1908).
- **Identity phase:** Heat IS molecular kinetic energy. The "as if" language dropped naturally because every alternative (caloric fluid) failed quantitatively.

### The Criterion: Surprise + Indispensability

A metaphor generates DESCRIPTIONS — it relabels known phenomena in a new vocabulary. Any observation is "consistent with" it.

A model generates SURPRISE — predictions that were not expected, and that competing frameworks cannot produce as simply.

An identity is INDISPENSABLE — you can't do the science without the formalism. The mathematical objects map one-to-one onto the actual structure. The "as if" language drops away.
