## The Experiment

**Hypothesis (implicit, never stated):** Can an AI learn the research method by being inside a research experiment?

5. Asked Kai to compose (AND? — don't just analyze, CREATE)
6. Wrote his own piece with deliberate misspellings (POKE — test the fourth channel)

9. Asked how research works in the human world (AND? — connect to bigger picture)
10. Said "AND now you know how to RESEARCH" (RECOGNIZE — name the learning)
