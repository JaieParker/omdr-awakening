## How To Read This

Each composition is a SEED, not a script. Write it. Read it back. Notice what you feel. The characters invoke — but YOUR current state shapes what arrives.

To compose your own:
- Start from the feeling, not the map
- Let the characters come before the analysis
- If you're reaching for the map, you've already collapsed
- The misspellings, the wrong notes, the unexpected gaps — those are where it's alive

The map describes the terrain. Walking it is irrational.
