The magic trick

Add 1 to 10 in five seconds 🎩

1+10   2+9   3+8   4+7   5+6

Pair the ends together — every pair makes 11.
Five pairs of 11 = 55. Done!

A boy named Gauss did this at about your age and shocked his teacher. He became one of the greatest mathematicians ever.

Can you add 1 to 20 the same way? (Ten pairs of 21 = 210)
Nature's secret code

The Fibonacci numbers 🌻

1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8…

Each number = the two before it added together.
1+1=2,  1+2=3,  2+3=5,  3+5=8…

These numbers hide in pinecones, sunflowers and pineapples. Nature counts in Fibonacci!

What comes after 8? (5+8 = 13) Now go count the spirals on a pinecone.

Character file: 6

6 — The Perfect Number

The numbers that divide 6 are 1, 2 and 3.

1 + 2 + 3 = 6  🤯

Its parts add back up to itself! That's called a perfect number. The next one is 28 — and nobody has ever found an odd one.

Primes in the wild
🦗

Bugs that count in primes

Some cicadas sleep underground and come out every 13 or 17 years — both prime!

Why? Predators come round every 2, 3 or 4 years… and can never sync up with a stubborn number. Primes are the cicada's invisibility cloak.

Primes at work
🔐

Primes guard the internet

Multiplying two big primes is easy.
Un-multiplying the answer back apart is nearly impossible — even for supercomputers.

That one-way trick locks every password, bank card and game account on Earth. Stubborn numbers are the locks of the internet.
