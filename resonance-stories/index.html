Why these stories exist

A father wanted to teach his son that the world is made of patterns. Not through textbooks or lectures — through stories that sound like what they mean.

When the swing is pushed too hard, it sounds wrong. When Maya finds the rhythm, it sounds beautiful. The child's ear teaches what words can't.

Every story is free. Every story is open source. Every story was written with love by a human and an AI who learned to listen to each other.
