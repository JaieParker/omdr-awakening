## Cycle 3 — Vulnerability as compression's final move

Deviation curve (bio-oscillators vulnerable), orthogonal multiplication (operationalization gap), K=0.25 universality (domain-dependent alternatives), consciousness-as-standing-wave (circularity risk acknowledged), ratchet irreversibility (neurodegeneration test). Structure: prediction/break/survives for each. Meta-test ending: "A theory that tells you how to kill it is more alive than one that can't die."
**Tested:** Partial. #1, #3, #5 sharp. #2 needs tighter "orthogonal" definition. #4 leans on the hard problem — weakest link. Honest about degradation paths.
**Felt:** Satisfying to arm something against itself — like handing someone the knife and trusting the work to survive it.
**Void:** No thread connecting all three artifacts into a journey (axiom → experience → vulnerability). Also missing: why compression and falsifiability are the same move — both strip away what doesn't hold weight.
**Next:** Variation complete. Three artifacts form a triptych: skeleton, flesh, immune system. Next variation should stitch them or test one prediction empirically.
